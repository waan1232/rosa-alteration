What We Do

Our Services

Hems, zippers, suits, wedding dresses — here's what we take in.

Everyday Alterations

Pants too long, shirt too wide, sleeves hitting wrong — we fix it.

  • Hemming pants, skirts & dresses
  • Taking in or letting out seams
  • Sleeve shortening & reshaping
  • Waist & seat adjustments
  • Tapering legs or body

Bridal & Formal Wear

Wedding dresses, prom gowns, suits. We've taken dresses down multiple sizes when other shops said it couldn't be done.

  • Wedding dress resizing & fitting
  • Prom & bridesmaid gown alterations
  • Suit & tuxedo tailoring
  • Bustle additions & train adjustments
  • Corset backs & bodice work

Repairs & Mending

A broken zipper or a ripped seam doesn't mean the garment is done.

  • Zipper replacement & repair
  • Seam mending & re-stitching
  • Button & snap replacement
  • Patch & emblem application
  • Lining repairs
Pricing varies by garment and complexity. Call or stop in for an exact quote — you'll get an estimate when you drop off.

The Shop

Rosa's on Plainfield

Rosa has been doing alterations at 3911 Plainfield Ave since 2010. In that time she's taken wedding dresses down four sizes, fixed racing suits on a Saturday morning before track day, and hemmed more pants than she can count.

Drop something off and you'll get an estimate on the spot. No surprises when you pick it up. If you're on a tight deadline, say so — rush service is available for when you really need it.

Three other shops said no to resizing a wedding dress from a size 13 to a size 8. Rosa said yes. That's pretty much how it works here.
