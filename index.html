Our Story

The Art of the Perfect Fit

At Rosa Alteration & Tailor, every stitch is placed with intention. For over fifteen years, we have served the Grand Rapids community from our studio on Plainfield Avenue — transforming garments into clothes that feel like they were made just for you.

Our approach begins with listening. We take time to understand how you wear your clothes, the occasions they're meant for, and the fit you're looking for. Then we get to work — methodically, precisely, and without shortcuts.

From emergency same-day zipper repairs to complex bridal resizing, we treat every garment — and every customer — with the same meticulous attention to detail.
